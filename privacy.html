Privacy policy

MAP Video Lab rclone (SSD) · Effective date: 18 September 2026

Purpose and data accessed

This is a personal desktop transfer and media-library workflow operated by the Google account owner. Depending on the permission granted, the tools access Google Drive file contents and metadata (including file IDs, names, parent folders, sizes and checksums), library-related Google Sheets cells, and OAuth credentials needed to perform the owner's requested operations.

Use of data

Data is used to retrieve project inputs, transfer and verify external video footage and its provenance records, and maintain the asset index. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by these transfer tools to train generalized AI models. Downstream video editing or AI applications are separate services used at the owner's direction and are outside the scope of this transfer application's operation.

Storage and disclosure

Library files and index data are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Authorized working copies and credentials are stored on owner-controlled local storage with access restrictions. Requests are sent to Google's OAuth, Drive and Sheets services. Credentials and private media are not included in this public website. The tools do not intentionally share private Drive content with unrelated third parties; disclosures required by applicable law may apply.

Retention, control and deletion

Cloud files remain until the owner deletes them. Local working copies, operation receipts and credentials remain until removed according to the owner's workflow. Access can be revoked at Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing cloud files or local copies. The owner can remove those separately.

Google API data policy

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This static information site has no application analytics, login form, tracking scripts or advertising. Its hosting provider may process standard web request information under its own policies.
